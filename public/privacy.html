3. Local Device Storage & Caching

ലോക്കൽ സ്റ്റോറേജ് കാഷിംഗ്

The application utilizes local device storage (AsyncStorage / LocalStorage / Service Worker) exclusively for offline usability and bandwidth conservation:

  • Daily Blessing (Varadan) & Murli Content: Cached locally so daily spiritual thoughts can be read even without an active internet connection.
  • Audio & Media References: Temporary caching to prevent repetitive network fetches and save mobile data quotas.
  • User Preferences: Audio playback settings and notification preferences saved strictly on your local device.
